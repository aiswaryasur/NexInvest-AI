# ⚡ NexInvest AI

### AI-Powered Multi-Agent Stock Analysis Platform

> **Turn market data into intelligent insights — powered by AI agents.**

NexInvest AI is a modern, AI-powered stock analysis dashboard designed to provide investors with a structured view of market conditions by combining **technical analysis, sentiment analysis, and AI-generated insights** into a single workflow.

The platform uses multiple AI agents to analyze a stock concurrently and synthesizes their outputs into a concise market signal.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 Multi-Agent AI Analysis

NexInvest AI uses specialized AI agents to analyze different aspects of a stock:

- 📈 **Technical Analyst**
  - Evaluates price trends and technical signals
  - Identifies potential market momentum
  - Produces structured analytical signals

- 💬 **Sentiment Analyst**
  - Analyzes news and market sentiment
  - Evaluates positive/negative momentum
  - Provides an independent perspective on market psychology
---

## 📊 Market Dashboard

The dashboard provides a real-time-style overview of a selected stock.

### Stock Overview

Displays:

- Current stock price
- Daily percentage change
- Day high
- Day low
- Exchange information

### 📈 Market Status

Provides broader market context through major indices such as:

- NIFTY 50
- SENSEX

### ⚡ Performance Metrics

The interface also highlights system-level metrics such as:

- Signal Accuracy
- Average AI Response Time
- Pipeline status
